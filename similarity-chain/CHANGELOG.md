# Changelog

## 2026-11-05

First public release, accompanying the arXiv preprint. The task catalog, the catalogs of conditions and dissimilarity functions and the report template follow the preprint's supplement.

Same day: the worked example now follows the preprint's running example, a meta-analysis of clozapine trials, and failure case 1 is now the coding revision in US mortality data from the preprint's supplement. Both replace older transport examples. Task 1.2 (Meta-analyze) now follows the running example, and the data and model catalogs add its conditions, evidence compatibility and synthesis adequacy. The catalogs also follow the supplement's wording for structural causal similarity, calibration and inclusion-function agreement, and add retention and purity for signature equivalence.

Same day, after a literature check of the catalogs: structural causal similarity is defined by parent sets and assessed with the structural Hamming distance, and effect similarity replaces probabilistic and full causal similarity. Calibration and discrimination are attributed to the model and the case mix. Distributional and missingness comparability carry a task-stated bound. The catalogs add small-sample caveats for standardized mean differences, REML for the between-study variance, and corrected examples.
